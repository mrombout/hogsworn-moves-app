To play Quidditch, start a scene challenge, setting the rank to Dangerous, unless the teams are seriously lopsided. Use _Face Danger_ and _Secure an Advantage_ using the appropriate stat. Not all rolls should be +edge of course; you could use heart to plunge towards the ground and pull up at the last second, commit a foul unseen using cunning, use power to knock a bludger into the opposing Seeker, etc.

When you _Finish the Scene_, the snitch has been caught and the game is over.

On a **strong hit**, your team wins.
On a **weak hit**, either your team loses but you played well, or your yeam wins, but must _Pay the Price_.
On a **miss**, you lose dramatically, _Pay the Price_.

Alternatively, for shorter games, you can use the _Battle_ move to resolve the match in one roll. Use the appropriate stat for your position as defined page 5. You could also roll +wits if you shared a strategy with the team and they use it (even if you aren't on the team), or if you are the team captain.
