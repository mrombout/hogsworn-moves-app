When you reach what you believe to be the
conclusion of a mystery, roll the challenge dice
and compare to your progress. Momentum is
ignored on this roll.

On a **strong hit**, your mystery is solved or your
quest complete. Mark experience (troublesome=1; dangerous=2; formidable=3;
extreme=4; epic=5).
On a **weak hit**, there is more to be done or you realize the truth of your quest. Envision what you discover (Ask the Oracle if unsure). Then, mark experience (troublesome=0; dangerous=1; formidable=2; extreme=3; epic=4). You may
Investigate a Mystery to set things right. If you do, add +1.
On a **miss**, your investigation or quest was in vain. Envision what happens (_Ask the Oracle_ if unsure), and choose one.
* You recommit: Clear all but one filled progress,
and raise the mystery’s rank by one (if not already epic).
* You give up: _Pay the Price_.
