When your momentum is at its minimum (-6), and you suffer additional -momentum, choose one.

* Exchange each additional -momentum for any combination of -health, -spirit, or -reputation as appropriate to the circumstances.
* Envision an event or discovery (_Ask the Oracle_ if unsure) which undermines your progress in a current quest, journey or fight. Then, for each additional -momentum, clear 1 unit of progress on that track per its rank.
