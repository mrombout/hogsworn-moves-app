When you are brought to the brink of desolation, roll +heart.

On a **strong hit**, you resist and press on.
On a **weak hit**, choose one.
* Your spirit or sanity breaks, but not before you make a noble sacrifice. Envision your final moments.
* You see a vision of a dreaded event coming to pass. Envision that dark future (_Ask the Oracle_ if unsure), and _Investigate a Mystery_ (formidable or extreme) to prevent it. If you fail to score a hit when you _Investigate a Mystery_, or refuse the request, you are lost. Otherwise, you return to your senses and are now  tormented. You may only clear the tormented debility by completing the quest.
On a **miss**, you succumb to despair or horror and are lost.
