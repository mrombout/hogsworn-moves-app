When you get caught and lose reputation, roll +reputation or +cunning, whichever is higher. If your reputation is 0, suffer -momentum equal to any remaining -reputation.

On a **strong hit**, pick one:
* They are lenient. Take -1 momentum for +1 reputation.
* It was worth it. Take +1 momentum.
On a **weak hit**, you are scolded, your house may lose a few points. Carry on.
On a **miss**, suffer -1 momentum. If you are at 0 rep, you must mark Notorious (if current unmarked) or roll on the following table.

| Roll   | Result                                                                        |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1-10   | You face expulsion, play out the scene with the Headmaster.                   |
| 11-25  | Your punishment is cruel and time-consuming, mark progress on the Time Track. |
| 26-50  | You get detention and are viewed as a troublemaker.                           |
| 51-100 | You only loose House Points.                                                  |
