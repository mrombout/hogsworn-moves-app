When you prepare a potion, write its name down and give it an appropriate rank. In general, students learn how to brew Dangerous potions starting in Year 3, Formidable in Year 5, and Extreme in Year 7. If a recipe has been researched, you can try brewing potions one rank higher at -2.

Envision which step you are taking (gathering ingredients, crushing, mixing, etc.), then, roll +cunning (skip creating a new track while you are still working on the same potion).

On a **strong hit**, mark progress. You manage to find the ingredients, or you successfully follow the correct brewing steps.
On a **weak hit**, you fail something, you breathe in a foul smell, you risk getting caught, or you burn yourself... Suffer -1 to momentum, spirit, health, or reputation.
On a **mis**, same as above, but you must choose to bottle your potion (you may no longer _Brew a Potion_ to progress on this track), or discard it.
