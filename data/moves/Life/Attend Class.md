Mark progress on time track, then roll the relevant skill. If you are studying a spell, you can roll to _Learn a spell_, even on a miss.

On a **strong hit**, mark progress on the Academic track, envision the scene and pick the most relevant:

* Add +1 if you roll to Learn a spell
* Brew a potion +1, using the ingredients provided. You get to keep it when it is done. (Take brewing length into consideration)
* Get +2 reputation
* Get +2 momentum
* Get +1 to _Forge a bond_ with teacher or partner

On a **weak hit**, class doesn’t go as planned. _Ask the Oracle_ to see what happened and play out the scene. If you manage to get things back on track, mark progress on the academic track.
On a **miss**, envision what went wrong and _Pay
the Price_.
