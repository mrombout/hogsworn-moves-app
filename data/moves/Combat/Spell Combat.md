When you use a move in combat by casting spells you Know. Proceed with the move as usual, but roll +relevant to the spell type (note you may use a single move to represent multiple spells, use the stat you feel most relevant if multiple types are used).

Cast Known spells at -1. Damaging spells cause 2 harm, whereas non-spell damage causes 1 harm.

Transfigurating an enemy is a rather powerful move, so consider doing so on your last enemy only when you are ready to _End the Fight_. You could only still use transfiguration strategically on other items or insuspecting animals to _Secure an Advantage_ or _Face Danger_, or if faces with several enemies.
