When you have initiative and attack on close quarters, roll +power. When you have initiative and attack at range, roll +edge. If using spells, use _Spell Combat_.

On a **strong hit**, inflict +1 harm. You retain initiative.
On a **weak hit**, inflict your harm and lose initiative.
On a **mis**, your attack fails and you must _Pay the Price_. Your foe has initiative.
