When you spend significant time with a person or community, stand together to face hardships, or make sacrifices for their cause, you can attempt to create a bond. When you do, roll +heart. If you make this move after you successfully _Solve a Mystery_ to their benefit, you may reroll any dice.

On a **strong hit**, make note of the bond, mark progress on your bond progress track, and choose one.
* Take +1 spirit
* Take +2 momentum
* Take +1 reputation (if bonded with a member of Hogwarts' staff.)
On a **weak hit**, they ask something more of you first.
Envision what it is (_Ask the Oracle_ if unsure), do it (or _Investigate a Mystery_), and mark the bond. If you refuse or fail, _Pay the Price_.
On a **mis**, they reject you. _Pay the Price_.
