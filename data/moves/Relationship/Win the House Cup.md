At the end of every school year, the House Cup winners are announced, roll the challenge dice and compare to your bonds. Momentum is ignored on this roll.

On a **strong hit**, your House has won the House Cup! Reset your spirit track and take +1 XP. Reset your bonds tracker to 0.
On a **weak hit**, your House put up a good effort. Start the next Year with 2 progress marked.
On a **miss**, your House is dead last. _Endure Stress_ (1), but don't reset your bonds tracker (keep 5 progress max).
