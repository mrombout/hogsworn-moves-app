When you rest in the warmth of your Common Roo, roll +Heart. If with someone with whom you share a bond, add +1.

On a **strong hit**, you and your allies may each pick two.
On a **weak hit**, you and your allies may each pick one:
* Recuperate: Take +health for you and any companions.
* Be Raucous: Suffer -1 reputation and take +2 spirit.
* Relax: Take +1 spirit.
* Focus: Take +1 momentum.
* Prepare: When you _Secure an Advantage_, add +1.
On a **miss**, something happens while you are in the common room, or as you are heading there. _Pay the Price_. Perhaps a housemate asks something of you or a mysterious even happens.
