When you use a potion you created, roll the challenge dice and compare to your progress on the Potion's track. Momentum is ignored on this roll.

On a **strong hit**, the potion works as intended.
On a **weak hit**, the potion mostly works, but it has a side effect or is less potent than intended. Take -1 momentum.
On a **miss**, the potion was not prepared as it should, something goes horribly wrong. _Pay the Price_.
