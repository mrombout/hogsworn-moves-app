This is the progress roll for the Academic track, momentum is ignored on this roll.
Reset your Health for the next year.

On a **strong hit**: Outstanding work!
* Mark +3XP. reset your reputation and spirit
* Mark 3 spells from the year you are ending as Learned 
* Mark 3 spells from the year you are starting as Known.
On a **weak hit**: You can do better. Pick one from above, then _Ensure Stress (1 stress)_.
On a **mis**: Dreadful results. _Get Caught_ -2, you should considering studying over summer.
Don't forget to check if you _Win the House Cup_ at the end of the year.
