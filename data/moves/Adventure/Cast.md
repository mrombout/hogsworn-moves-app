When you cast a spell you Know outside of combat, roll +relevant skill. Take -2 for each year above, or +1 for each year below. You don't need to roll for _learned_ spells 3 years below your own \[Y4 -> Y1]. Add +2 if the spell is Learned.

If you cast a **Charm**, roll +Edge.
If you cast a **Defensive or Restorative Spell**, roll +Heart.
If you cast **Dark Arts**: Jinxes (instant effect), Hexes (short-term effect), or Curses (long-term effect), roll +Power.
If you cast **Transfiguration spell** (very short range), roll +Wits.

On a **strong hit**, the spell is successful, envision what happens and gain +1 momentum.
You may use the occasion to _Learn a Spell_.
On a **weak hit**, you cast the spell, but (pick one)...
* The effect isn't as powerful as it should be.
* The effect doesn't last as long as it should.
* You attract unwanted attention.
On a **miss**, _Pay the Price_. The spell misses (lose momentum), backfires (_Endure Harm or Stress_), draws attention (_Get Caught_)...
