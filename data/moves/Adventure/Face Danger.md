When you attempt something risky or react to an imminent threat, envision your action and roll. If you act...

* With speed, agility, or precision: Roll +edge.
* With charm, loyalty, or courage: Roll +heart.
* With aggressive action, forceful defense, strength, or endurance: Roll +power.
* With deception, stealth, or trickery: Roll +cunning.
* With expertise, insight, or observation: Roll +wits.

On a **strong hit**, you are successful. Take +1 momentum.
On a **weak hit**, you succeed, but face a troublesome cost. Choose one:
- You are delayed, loose advantage, or face a new danger: Suffer -1 momentum.
- You are tired or hurt: _Endure Harm_ (1 harm).
- You are dispirited or afraid: _Endure Stress_ (1 stress).
- You _Get Caught_ (1 reputation).
On a **mis**, you fail, or your progress is undermined by a dramatic and costly turn of events. _Pay the Price_.
