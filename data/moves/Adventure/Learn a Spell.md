When you learn a spell in class, with a mentor or through your own means, or when you practice a spell you Know, roll +relevant skill. Take -2 for each year above, or +1 for each your below.

On a **strong hit**, choose: Mark a Known spell as Learned and get +2 momentum, or mark an unknown spell as Learned.
On a **weak hit**, choose: Mark a Known spell as Learned but take -2 momentum, or mark an unknown spell as Known.
On a **mis**, _Pay the Price_. The spell backfires spectacularly, you get frustrated, you get caught learning a forbidden spell...
