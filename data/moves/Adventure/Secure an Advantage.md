When you assess a situation, make preparations, or attempt to gain leverage, envision your action and roll. If you act...

* With speed, agility, or precision: Roll +edge.
* With charm, loyalty, or courage: Roll +heart.
* With aggressive action, forceful defense, strength, or endurance: Roll +power.
* With deception, stealth, trickery, or divination: Roll +cunning.
* With expertise, insight, or observation: Roll +wits.

On a **strong hit**, take both. One a **weak hit**, choose one.
* Take +2 momentum.
* Add +1 on your next move (not a progress move).
On a **mis**, you fail or your assumptions betray you. _Pay the Price_.
