This is the progress roll for the Time track, momentum is ignored on this roll.

When the track reaches 10, prepare to make this roll.
Move on to a new Time Track.

On a **strong hit**, pick two, on a **weak hit**, pick one:
- Gain +2 health, +2 spirit, or +2 reputation.
- Remove a debility and take +1 health, spirit, or reputation.
- Mark 1 spell in each category from your year as known.
- Gain 1 XP.
- On a **mis**, _Pay the Price_. You have a wretched time in Hogwarts, something troubles you...
